## Deep Lock Feature

### Accessibility Service

Deep Lock uses the Android Accessibility Service. By enabling this feature, you acknowledge:

- During an active Focus session, Deep Lock reads the package name and display name of the app that comes to the foreground to determine whether it is on your allowed list.
- Student OS stores the allowed-app list and blocked-app attempts on this device. A blocked-app record can include the app package name and display name, attempt time, Deep Lock mode, remaining focus time, and focus-session ID.
- The service does not read or store screen text, messages, notifications, passwords, typed text, or app window content. Accessibility-derived information is not transmitted to or shared with third parties.
- You can disable Deep Lock at any time.

### Foreground Service

Deep Lock uses a Foreground Service to maintain functionality when the App is in the background. This service:

- Shows a persistent notification during active Focus Sessions.
- Starts when a Focus Session begins and stops when it ends.
- Can be disabled by not using Deep Lock.

### Limitation of Effectiveness

Deep Lock is provided as a tool to help you maintain focus. We cannot guarantee:

- That it will block all distracting apps in all situations.
- That it will prevent you from accessing blocked apps through other means.
- That it will work identically across all Android devices and versions.

Deep Lock is a voluntary tool. You are ultimately responsible for your own focus and productivity.
