## [1.2.0] - 2026-10-18

First public release. 🎉

### Added

- **Focus mode** — drift-free, timestamp-based timer with presets, custom duration and an ambient soundscape.
- **Tasks** — list & kanban views, priorities, subjects, due dates, subtasks and XP rewards.
- **Habits** — weekly grid, streak mechanics and celebration animations.
- **Planner** — month / week / day views, syllabus tracking and exam countdown.
- **Analytics** — focus trends, subject breakdown and productivity insights.
- **Deep Lock** — Android Focus Shield that blocks distracting apps during a session.
- **Command palette** — ⌘K / Ctrl+K access to every view and action.
- **Themes** — light, dark and AMOLED, with accent-colour and font-size options.
- **PWA** — installable, offline-capable, with a service worker and manifest.
- **Android app** — Capacitor shell with native alarm scheduling and Focus Shield plugins.
- **Local-first persistence** — Zustand (`localStorage`) with Dexie (IndexedDB) helpers.
